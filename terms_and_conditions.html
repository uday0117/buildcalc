3. Description of Service

BuildCalc is a construction calculator application that provides:

  • Cement Calculator: Calculate cement bags needed for construction
  • Brick Calculator: Estimate number of bricks required
  • Sand Calculator: Calculate sand quantity in cubic feet or cubic meters
  • Paint Calculator: Estimate paint quantity for walls
  • Steel Calculator: Calculate steel/rebar requirements
  • Tile Calculator: Estimate tiles needed for flooring/walls
  • Area Calculator: Calculate areas of various shapes
  • Cost Estimator: Estimate construction costs
  • Calculation History: Save and view past calculations
