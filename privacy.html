Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 18, 2026

BuildCalc is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

BuildCalc may collect the following information that you provide directly:

  • Calculation inputs (measurements, dimensions, quantities)
  • Saved calculations and history
  • User preferences and settings

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash reports and performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: To perform calculations and save your data locally
  • Improve App: To enhance functionality and user experience
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide assistance
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the app

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All your calculations, history, and saved data are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your calculation data on external servers.

3.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Third-Party Services

BuildCalc may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

5. Children's Privacy

BuildCalc is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data stored in the app
  • Delete your calculation history and saved data
  • Opt-out of analytics tracking
  • Request information about data we may have collected

7. Data Retention

Your calculation data is retained locally on your device until you choose to delete it. You can clear your history and saved calculations at any time through the app settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."
