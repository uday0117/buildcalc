Privacy Policy for BuildCalc

Last Updated: January 19, 2025

Introduction

UK Solutions ("us", "we", or "our") operates the BuildCalc mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information.

BuildCalc is a construction calculator application that performs calculations locally on your device. We do not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your usage
  • Store your calculation history on our servers
  • Transmit data to our servers
  • Use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Require account registration
  • Request any permissions beyond what's necessary for the app to function

Data Storage

All calculations and saved data are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. No calculation data is stored permanently on external servers or transmitted to any server. The app only stores your:

  • Calculation history (locally on your device)
  • Saved calculations (locally on your device)
  • App preferences and settings (locally on your device)

Third-Party Services

BuildCalc uses minimal third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: Required for app distribution. Google Play's privacy policy applies.
  • Flutter Framework: Open-source UI framework by Google.

These services do not collect information used to identify you.

Permissions

BuildCalc requires minimal permissions:

  • Internet: Only used to open external links (Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions) in a browser.
  • Storage: To save your calculations and history locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing calculation history within the app
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all locally stored data)
  • Clearing app data from your device settings

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
